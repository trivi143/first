Battlegrounds Mobile India

Battlegrounds Mobile India (BGMI) is a free-to-play battle royale multiplayer game developed and published by Krafton, designed specifically for players in India. Essentially, it is the Indian version of PUBG Mobile.

Gameplay Core

  1. The Premise: Up to 100 players parachute onto a remote island. Players must locate and scavenge their own weapons, armor, and supplies while eliminating opponents
  2. The Blue Zone: The playable area of the map decreases in size over time, forcing surviving players into tighter encounters. The last standing individual or squad wins the match (the iconic "Chicken Dinner").
  3. Maps Available: The game features diverse terrains including classic maps like Erangel, Miramar, Sanhok, Vikendi, Livik, Karakin, and Nusa.
